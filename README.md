## 🌟 Key Features Built-In

1. **High-Contrast "Kinetic Apex" Aesthetic**:
   - Deep Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A`) paired with High-Voltage Electric Lime (`#BEF500` / `#C6FF00`).
   - Disciplined zero-radius (`0px`) industrial geometry inspired by laser-cut performance fabrics.
   - Micro-telemetry pulse indicators and hairline architectural borders.

2. **Complete Product Catalog & Interactive Showcase**:
   - 8 Core Product Categories (Sportswear, Gym & Fitness, Men's, Women's, Sublimated Teamwear, Tracksuits, Compression, Outerwear).
   - Filterable production showcase (Tops & Tees, Bottoms & Tights, Outerwear).
   - Detailed technical spec modal with segmented gauge bars for **Compression**, **Breathability**, and **Durability**.

3. **Interactive B2B Cost & MOQ Estimator**:
   - Real-time pricing engine calculating unit FOB cost and total production investment based on quantity (50 to 3,000+ units) and embellishment selections (Silicone 3D, Sublimation, Woven trims).
   - "Transfer Specs to Official RFQ" automatically populates the inquiry form.

4. **Production Protocol Timeline**:
   - 5-stage disciplined manufacturing pipeline: Submit Specs ➔ Material Lock ➔ Sample Sign-Off ➔ Bulk Production ➔ Global DDP Shipping.

5. **Industrial B2B RFQ Form with Tech Pack Uploader & Web3Forms Integration**:
   - Integrated with Web3Forms Access Key (`ebf1fa59-544f-423b-ac3c-97ed0f85019e`) for instant email delivery to your inbox upon form submission.
   - Drag-and-drop support for PDF, AI, PSD, and ZIP tech packs.
   - Built-in NDA confidentiality assurance and simulated generation of authentic order tracking IDs (e.g. `GB-2026-9842`).
   - Downloadable official quotation receipt (.txt) and instant WhatsApp follow-up link.

6. **Global Export & Communication Ready**:
   - Integrated WhatsApp floating button and contact shortcuts.
   - Sialkot Industrial Textiles Export Zone credentials, ISO 9001:2015, OEKO-TEX 100, and WRAP certifications.
   - Mobile responsive navigation drawer.
